## Before production use
1. Locate each original Unsplash photo page and identify photographer, source URL, publication/license classification and download date.
2. Confirm the photo is under the standard Unsplash license rather than Unsplash+ or another license.
3. Review visible logos, recognizable people, and third-party copyrighted material; obtain any additional releases needed.
4. Download authorized original image files, retain local copies in the private production repository (for example public/images/), and record file hashes, photographer and license provenance.
5. Retain a dated copy of the applicable license terms and a source-page record where legally and technically permissible.
6. Serve optimized local copies rather than depending on remote image hotlinks.
